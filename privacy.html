Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

CheatSheet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Notes, preferences, and the selected widget note are stored locally in the app's sandbox and Apple App Group container. CheatSheet has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs, or network service. The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other sensitive system data.

Deleting the app removes its local app data according to the platform's normal behavior. Content may remain in operating-system backups controlled by the user and Apple.

The source code is publicly available under the MIT License. Privacy-relevant changes can be reviewed in this repository. Questions or issues may be submitted through the repository's public issue tracker.
