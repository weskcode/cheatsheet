Notes & commands, at hand.

An open-source cheat-sheet app that keeps the commands and checklists you use every day one glance away.

View source on GitHub Privacy policy

Plain text, real structure

Type headings, bullets, and checkboxes in plain text — they format automatically, with no syntax to learn.

Pin one note to a widget

Keep the command you always forget on your Home Screen, menu bar, or desktop — visible without opening the app.

Color-code by project

Ten note colors and four font styles, including a monospaced default built for code.

Nothing leaves your device

No account, no ads, no analytics, and zero network calls. Everything is stored locally — verifiable, because it's open source.

Forgiving trash

Deleted notes wait 30 days in Trash. Restore them, or clear them immediately — your call.

Native on every device

Compact on iPhone, split-view on iPad and Mac, with a menu bar quick-capture on macOS.
